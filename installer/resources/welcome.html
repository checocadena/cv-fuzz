CV Fuzz

A fuzz plugin and interactive resume by Checo Cadena.

This is a C++ model of the fuzz pedal I designed and built during my time at NYU. It features asymmetric clipping, a starvable bias stage, 4x oversampling and a tone control. The interface is my resume. Each knob shapes the sound and opens up a different part of my resume.

This installs the Audio Unit, VST3 and standalone app. You can choose which under Customize.
