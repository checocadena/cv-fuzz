DESCRIPTION (one paragraph)

CV Fuzz is a fuzz plugin whose interface is Checo Cadena's resume. Its sound is a C++ model of a fuzz pedal Checo designed and built at NYU with NYU grant funding: a bias stage that can be starved like a dying battery, up to +35.7 dB of drive, an asymmetric clipper that runs at 4 times the sample rate, a DC blocker, a tone control and an output level. Each of its four knobs also opens one section of the CV: Fuzz opens Projects, Tone opens Experience, Level opens Education and Bias opens Recognition. The name is a pun, since in modular synthesis CV means control voltage. CV Fuzz is built with JUCE 8 as an Audio Unit, VST3 and standalone app for macOS, and as a VST3 and standalone app for Windows and Linux. Version 1.0.1 is a free download from GitHub, and the source code is MIT licensed.


SHORT BIO

Checo Cadena is a New York-based singer-songwriter, sound artist and creative technologist from Monterrey, Mexico, who writes and produces records and builds the machines around them: plugins, robots, installations and software. Since 2023 Cadena has been Computer Control Engineer at Amorphic Robot Works, Chico MacMurtrie's robotic sculpture studio. Cadena holds a B.S. in Integrated Design and Media from NYU Tandon and an M.A. in Creative Media and Technology from Berklee NYC.
